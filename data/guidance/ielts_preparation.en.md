# IELTS preparation (sample notes - replace with your own)

Take one full practice test first to find your weakest skill. Plan your study time around that skill instead of studying everything equally.

Practise writing with a timer and ask a teacher or a strong English speaker to mark it. Practise speaking aloud by recording yourself.

Book the exam early, because test dates fill up and many scholarships need the result before the application deadline. Keep a retake buffer in your budget.
