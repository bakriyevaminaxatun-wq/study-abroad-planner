# Motivation letter (sample notes - replace with your own)

Write the letter yourself. It must show your own story, goals and reasons. Tools and friends can give feedback, but the content has to be yours.

A simple structure: why this field, what you have already done, why this programme or country, what you will do after the scholarship.

Keep to the word limit, use concrete examples instead of general praise, and read the scholarship's selection criteria before writing.
