# Recommendation letters (sample notes - replace with your own)

Ask early. Choose people who know your work well, such as a thesis supervisor or a manager, and not only people with famous titles.

Give each referee your CV, a short note about the scholarship, the deadline and the list of things you hope they can mention. Send a polite reminder one week before the deadline.

Check the scholarship rules: some require a form or an online submission by the referee, and some do not accept letters sent by the applicant.
